1. What is the fire triangle made up of?
A. Smoke, heat and fuel
B. Heat, fuel and oxygen
C. Water, oxygen and smoke
D. Fuel, smoke and carbon dioxide
Answer: B

2. Which action is most effective for preventing a small electrical fire from becoming worse?
A. Pour water on it
B. Increase ventilation
C. Isolate the electrical supply if safe to do so
D. Cover it with paper
Answer: C

3. Which extinguisher is generally suitable for a fire involving ordinary combustible materials such as paper and wood?
A. Water
B. CO₂ only
C. Wet chemical only
D. None
Answer: A

4. Why should water generally not be used on energized electrical equipment?
A. It produces smoke
B. It may conduct electricity and cause electric shock
C. It makes the equipment heavier
D. It removes oxygen
Answer: B

5. What does PASS commonly stand for when operating a portable fire extinguisher?
A. Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep
B. Push, Alert, Stop, Spray
C. Pull, Alert, Stop, Sweep
D. Press, Aim, Spray, Stop
Answer: A

6. Which type of extinguisher is commonly appropriate for flammable liquid fires?
A. Water only
B. Foam
C. Water mist only
D. None
Answer: B

7. What should you do first when discovering a fire in a workplace?
A. Hide the fire
B. Raise the alarm and alert others
C. Search for your belongings
D. Open all doors and windows
Answer: B

8. Why should fire doors normally be kept closed?
A. To make the building quieter
B. To prevent people from entering
C. To help limit the spread of fire and smoke
D. To reduce electricity consumption
Answer: C

9. What is the safest action if a fire is rapidly increasing in size?
A. Fight it alone
B. Get closer to identify the flames
C. Evacuate and call the emergency services
D. Look for a larger extinguisher
Answer: C

10. Which fuel would be considered a flammable liquid?
A. Petrol
B. Wood
C. Paper
D. Cotton
Answer: A

11. Why can smoke be more dangerous than the flames during a fire?
A. Smoke is always hotter
B. Smoke can contain toxic gases and reduce available oxygen
C. Smoke cannot be seen
D. Smoke always causes explosions
Answer: B

12. What should you do if your clothing catches fire?
A. Run as fast as possible
B. Stop, drop and roll
C. Jump into traffic
D. Continue working
Answer: B

13. What is the purpose of a fire evacuation assembly point?
A. To store fire extinguishers
B. To provide a safe location where evacuated people can be accounted for
C. To allow workers to return to the building
D. To store equipment
Answer: B

14. Which of the following can increase the severity of a workplace fire?
A. Good housekeeping
B. Proper storage of flammable materials
C. Accumulation of combustible waste
D. Functional fire alarms
Answer: C

15. A worker discovers a small fire but has not received fire-extinguisher training. What should the worker do?
A. Attempt to fight the fire anyway
B. Raise the alarm and evacuate
C. Use any available liquid
D. Remove the fire alarm
Answer: B

16. What is the first priority when approaching an injured person?
A. Give them water
B. Check that the scene is safe
C. Move them immediately
D. Give medication
Answer: B

17. What should you do first when you find an unresponsive person?
A. Give them food
B. Check responsiveness and breathing and activate emergency help
C. Sit them upright
D. Give them medicine
Answer: B

18. What is the main purpose of CPR?
A. To stop bleeding
B. To restore circulation and support breathing when a person is unresponsive and not breathing normally
C. To treat broken bones
D. To reduce fever
Answer: B

19. What should you do for severe external bleeding?
A. Apply firm direct pressure to the wound
B. Wash the wound for 30 minutes
C. Remove all blood clots
D. Give the person food
Answer: A

20. What is the best immediate treatment for a minor thermal burn?
A. Apply butter
B. Apply toothpaste
C. Cool the burn with cool running water
D. Break any blisters
Answer: C

21. What should you NOT do to a burn?
A. Cool it with cool running water
B. Protect it from further injury
C. Apply butter, oil or toothpaste
D. Seek medical assistance when necessary
Answer: C

22. What is the correct action if a person is choking but can still cough forcefully?
A. Encourage them to continue coughing
B. Give them water immediately
C. Perform CPR immediately
D. Hit them repeatedly without assessment
Answer: A

23. What is a possible sign of shock?
A. Pale, cold and clammy skin
B. Increased appetite
C. Bright red skin in all cases
D. Normal breathing and complete alertness
Answer: A

24. What should a first aider do when treating an open wound?
A. Use dirty materials
B. Wear appropriate protective equipment where available
C. Touch the wound unnecessarily
D. Pour chemicals into the wound
Answer: B

25. Why is personal protective equipment such as gloves important during first aid?
A. It makes treatment faster
B. It protects the first aider and injured person from infection and contamination
C. It prevents all injuries
D. It replaces hand hygiene
Answer: B

26. What should you do if you suspect a person has a spinal injury?
A. Move them unnecessarily
B. Ask them to walk
C. Keep them as still as possible and seek emergency medical assistance
D. Give them food
Answer: C

27. Which of the following may indicate a fracture?
A. Abnormal shape or deformity of a limb
B. Increased appetite
C. Normal movement without pain
D. Improved flexibility
Answer: A

28. What should you do when someone has fainted but is breathing normally?
A. Give them alcohol
B. Place them in a safe position and monitor them
C. Force them to stand immediately
D. Give them medication
Answer: B

29. What is the purpose of a first-aid box in a workplace?
A. To replace professional medical care
B. To provide basic equipment for immediate treatment of injuries and illness
C. To store workers' personal medicines
D. To store cleaning chemicals
Answer: B

30. When should a first aider call for professional medical assistance?
A. Only when the injury looks serious
B. Whenever the person's condition requires medical assessment or emergency treatment
C. Only after giving first aid for one hour
D. Never, because first aid is enough
Answer: B
