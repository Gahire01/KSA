1. What is the main purpose of Occupational Safety and Health?
A. To increase production at any cost
B. To prevent work-related injuries, illnesses, and fatalities
C. To reduce the number of workers
D. To make workers wear PPE at all times
Answer: B

2. On a construction site, a worker notices that a scaffold has no proper guardrails. What should the worker do?
A. Use it carefully
B. Use it only for a short task
C. Report the condition and prevent its use until it is made safe
D. Ask another worker to use it first
Answer: C

3. Which of the following is a common hazard in mining operations?
A. Ground instability
B. Office paperwork
C. Computer screens
D. Filing cabinets
Answer: A

4. Before working on electrical equipment that could become energized, what is an important safety measure?
A. Wear ordinary gloves
B. Switch off the lights only
C. Isolate the energy source and apply appropriate lockout/tagout procedures
D. Work quickly before the equipment becomes hot
Answer: C

5. What is a near miss?
A. An event that could have caused injury or damage but did not
B. A minor injury requiring first aid
C. A planned safety inspection
D. A medical examination
Answer: A

6. A worker falls from a ladder but is not seriously injured. What should happen?
A. Nothing because there is no serious injury
B. The incident should be reported and investigated
C. The worker should return immediately to work
D. The ladder should simply be put back
Answer: B

7. Which control is generally the most effective according to the hierarchy of controls?
A. PPE
B. Warning signs
C. Administrative controls
D. Elimination
Answer: D

8. A construction worker is required to work near an open edge several metres above the ground. Which control provides the best protection?
A. Telling the worker to be careful
B. Providing a guardrail or other suitable fall-prevention system
C. Giving the worker a mobile phone
D. Reducing the work period to five minutes
Answer: B

9. Which of the following is a serious electrical hazard?
A. Damaged electrical insulation exposing live conductors
B. A clean electrical room
C. A labelled distribution board
D. A properly maintained cable
Answer: A

10. Why is housekeeping important at a workplace?
A. It only improves appearance
B. It helps control hazards such as slips, trips, falling objects, and blocked access
C. It eliminates the need for PPE
D. It replaces safety inspections
Answer: B

11. In an underground mine, workers report unusual ground movement. What is the safest response?
A. Continue working until the end of the shift
B. Investigate the condition and follow the mine's ground-control and emergency procedures
C. Ignore it unless rocks start falling
D. Ask workers to work faster
Answer: B

12. What does PPE stand for?
A. Personal Protective Equipment
B. Personal Prevention Environment
C. Professional Protection Equipment
D. Public Protective Environment
Answer: A

13. A worker is repeatedly exposed to high levels of noise in a factory. What is the best approach?
A. Ignore it if the worker is used to the noise
B. Assess the exposure and apply appropriate controls, including hearing protection where necessary
C. Tell the worker to cover their ears with their hands
D. Move the worker without investigating the source
Answer: B

14. Before using a chemical at work, which information should workers understand?
A. Only its price
B. Its hazards, safe handling, storage, and emergency information
C. The company's profit from selling it
D. The supervisor's personal opinion
Answer: B

15. An electrician is asked to repair a circuit, but there is no confirmation that the circuit has been isolated. What should the electrician do?
A. Start work carefully
B. Touch the cable with a tool to check
C. Confirm proper isolation and absence of electrical energy before starting work
D. Ask another worker to hold the cable
Answer: C

16. What is an LTI?
A. Long-Term Inspection
B. Lost Time Injury
C. Labour Training Instruction
D. Limited Technical Incident
Answer: B

17. A crane is lifting a heavy load on a construction project. Several workers are standing underneath the suspended load. What is the safest action?
A. Allow them to stay if they are wearing helmets
B. Ask them to move away from the suspended load and establish an exclusion zone
C. Continue because the crane operator can see them
D. Tell them to move only if the load starts swinging
Answer: B

18. What is one major risk associated with poor manual handling?
A. Musculoskeletal injuries
B. Improved physical fitness
C. Better concentration
D. Reduced fatigue
Answer: A

19. A worker is found working at height without appropriate fall protection. The supervisor says, "Let him finish; he only has five minutes left." What is the correct response?
A. Allow the work because it is almost finished
B. Stop the unsafe work and ensure appropriate controls are in place
C. Ask the worker to work faster
D. Record the issue after the work is completed
Answer: B

20. Which of the following can be a significant hazard during blasting operations in mining?
A. Flyrock and uncontrolled blast effects
B. Office lighting
C. Clean drinking water
D. Safety meetings
Answer: A

21. What should a worker do when they discover a serious and immediate workplace hazard?
A. Ignore it
B. Report it and take appropriate action to prevent exposure to the danger
C. Wait until the monthly safety meeting
D. Photograph it and continue working
Answer: B

22. During a fire emergency, what should workers normally do?
A. Return to collect personal belongings
B. Follow the emergency procedure and evacuate to the designated assembly point
C. Hide in the nearest room
D. Use any available exit regardless of the emergency plan
Answer: B

23. A mining company has experienced several minor ground-fall incidents but has not investigated them because nobody was injured. What is the main concern?
A. There is no concern because nobody was injured
B. The incidents may indicate a serious underlying ground-control problem
C. Workers should simply be more careful
D. The incidents should not be recorded
Answer: B

24. Which of the following is an example of an ergonomic hazard?
A. Poor workstation design and prolonged awkward posture
B. Electrical shock
C. Explosives
D. Fire
Answer: A

25. After a serious workplace incident, what should an investigation primarily aim to establish?
A. Who should be blamed
B. The immediate and underlying causes and how recurrence can be prevented
C. How to avoid reporting the incident
D. Which worker should be dismissed
Answer: B

26. A worker notices that an electrical extension cable has damaged insulation. What should the worker do?
A. Continue using it carefully
B. Cover the damage temporarily with paper
C. Remove it from service and report it for proper repair or replacement
D. Give it to another worker
Answer: C

27. Which statement about safety induction is correct?
A. It is only necessary for new HSE officers
B. It helps workers understand workplace hazards, rules, emergency arrangements, and safe working practices
C. It replaces all other safety training
D. It is only required after an accident
Answer: B

28. A worker has been working long hours for several consecutive days and begins making repeated mistakes. What is the most likely safety concern?
A. Improved productivity
B. Fatigue increasing the likelihood of human error and incidents
C. Increased competence
D. Better decision-making
Answer: B

29. A company has written safety procedures, but supervisors regularly ignore them to meet production targets. What does this situation demonstrate?
A. Strong safety leadership
B. A poor safety culture and a gap between procedures and actual practice
C. Effective risk management
D. Excellent production management
Answer: B

30. A worker sees another employee entering a restricted electrical area without authorization. What is the most appropriate response?
A. Ignore it because it is not their responsibility
B. Allow the person to continue if they are experienced
C. Stop or report the unsafe access according to the workplace procedure
D. Wait until an accident occurs
Answer: C
