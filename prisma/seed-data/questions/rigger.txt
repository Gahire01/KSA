1. Before starting a lifting operation, what should the rigger do first?
A. Attach the sling immediately
B. Check the load, lifting plan, equipment, surroundings, and hazards
C. Ask the crane operator to start lifting
D. Move workers away without checking the load
Answer: B

2. A load is heavier than the capacity of the available sling. What should the rigger do?
A. Use the sling carefully
B. Use two workers to support the load
C. Select suitable lifting equipment with adequate capacity
D. Lift the load slowly
Answer: C

3. Why must the weight of a load be confirmed before lifting?
A. To know how many workers can watch the lift
B. To ensure the crane and rigging equipment have adequate capacity
C. To determine the color of the sling
D. To reduce the lifting time
Answer: B

4. During inspection, a wire rope sling has broken wires and severe deformation. What should you do?
A. Use it for a light load
B. Use it once and then replace it
C. Remove it from service and report it
D. Cover the damaged area with tape
Answer: C

5. What is the safest position for workers when a load is suspended?
A. Directly underneath the load
B. Close to the load so they can catch it
C. Outside the potential fall and swing zone
D. Between the load and a structure
Answer: C

6. Why is standing under a suspended load prohibited?
A. It distracts the crane operator
B. The load could fall or move unexpectedly and cause serious injury or death
C. It damages the crane
D. It increases the load weight
Answer: B

7. A load begins to rotate during lifting. What is the safest response?
A. Grab the load by hand
B. Stand underneath it and push it
C. Stop or control the lift from a safe position using appropriate means such as a tag line
D. Run toward the load
Answer: C

8. What is the main purpose of a tag line?
A. To increase lifting capacity
B. To control load movement and rotation from a safe position
C. To replace the sling
D. To hold the load permanently
Answer: B

9. A synthetic sling is being used around a sharp-edged steel plate. What should be done?
A. Use the sling without protection
B. Put the sling directly over the sharp edge
C. Use suitable edge protection
D. Wet the sling first
Answer: C

10. Why is the center of gravity important when rigging a load?
A. It helps determine how the load will balance and behave during lifting
B. It determines the crane color
C. It determines the lifting speed only
D. It reduces the load weight
Answer: A

11. A load starts tilting immediately after being lifted a short distance. What should the rigger do?
A. Continue because the load has already left the ground
B. Ask workers to hold the load
C. Stop, lower the load safely, and correct the rigging arrangement
D. Increase the lifting speed
Answer: C

12. What should happen before making a critical or complex lift?
A. Workers should begin immediately
B. The lift should be properly planned and hazards assessed
C. The crane should be tested by lifting the maximum load
D. The rigger should decide while the load is already suspended
Answer: B

13. What should a rigger do if the crane operator cannot clearly see the load or signal person?
A. Continue giving unclear signals
B. Use mobile phone communication while standing under the load
C. Establish an effective communication method before continuing
D. Ask several workers to give different signals
Answer: C

14. If the signal person gives a STOP signal during lifting, what should the crane operator do?
A. Ignore it if the load is light
B. Stop the operation
C. Increase the lifting speed
D. Ask why after completing the lift
Answer: B

15. Why should only one designated signal person normally direct the crane operator?
A. To avoid conflicting instructions and maintain clear communication
B. To reduce the number of workers
C. To make the lift faster
D. To give the operator less responsibility
Answer: A

16. A shackle has no visible identification or capacity marking. What is the safest action?
A. Estimate its capacity
B. Use it for a small load
C. Do not use it until its suitability and rated capacity are verified
D. Use two shackles instead
Answer: C

17. What can happen if a sling is loaded beyond its rated capacity?
A. Nothing if the lift is short
B. The sling may fail and the load may fall
C. The sling becomes stronger
D. The crane automatically compensates
Answer: B

18. Why should knots not be used to shorten or repair lifting slings?
A. They make the sling look untidy
B. They can significantly affect the sling's strength and safe performance
C. They increase the load capacity
D. They make the sling heavier
Answer: B

19. Before lifting, workers are found inside the exclusion zone. What should the rigger do?
A. Start the lift carefully
B. Warn them after lifting begins
C. Stop the operation and ensure the area is clear
D. Ask them to stay still
Answer: C

20. What is the purpose of an exclusion zone during lifting?
A. To keep unauthorized or exposed personnel away from lifting hazards
B. To provide a parking area
C. To store rigging equipment
D. To improve crane appearance
Answer: A

21. Strong wind starts affecting a large suspended load. What should the lifting team do?
A. Continue because the load is already suspended
B. Assess the conditions and stop the lift if it is no longer safe
C. Ask workers to hold the load
D. Increase crane speed
Answer: B

22. Why should a load not be dragged sideways using a crane unless specifically planned and permitted by the equipment manufacturer and lifting procedure?
A. It can create dangerous side loading and instability
B. It saves too much time
C. It makes the load lighter
D. It improves crane capacity
Answer: A

23. What is the safest way to position yourself while guiding a suspended load?
A. Between the load and a fixed structure
B. Where you could be trapped if the load moves
C. In a safe position with an escape route and outside the line of fire
D. Directly underneath the load
Answer: C

24. Why should a rigger keep an escape route available during lifting?
A. To leave work early
B. To move quickly to a safe position if the load shifts or an unexpected event occurs
C. To allow workers to walk under the load
D. To make communication easier
Answer: B

25. A lifting hook is damaged or has a visible crack. What should happen?
A. Use it for lighter loads
B. Paint the crack
C. Remove it from service and have it properly assessed
D. Continue if the safety latch works
Answer: C

26. What should be checked when selecting lifting accessories?
A. Capacity, condition, compatibility, and suitability for the load
B. Color only
C. Manufacturer's logo only
D. Length only
Answer: A

27. Why should a trial lift be performed before raising a load fully?
A. To check balance, rigging security, stability, and equipment response
B. To entertain workers
C. To increase crane capacity
D. To avoid using a lifting plan
Answer: A

28. During a lift, the rigger notices that the load is snagged against a structure. What should be done?
A. Increase the crane's lifting force
B. Ask workers to pull the load by hand
C. Stop the lift and safely remove the obstruction or reassess the lift
D. Continue until the load breaks free
Answer: C

29. What should a rigger do if conditions change and the planned lifting operation is no longer safe?
A. Continue because the lifting plan has already been approved
B. Stop the operation and reassess the hazards and controls
C. Allow the crane operator to decide alone
D. Complete the lift as quickly as possible
Answer: B

30. Which statement best represents good rigging safety?
A. Lift quickly and avoid delays
B. Trust experienced workers without inspection
C. Plan the lift, inspect equipment, use competent personnel, control the load, and keep people out of danger zones
D. Use additional workers whenever the equipment capacity is insufficient
Answer: C
